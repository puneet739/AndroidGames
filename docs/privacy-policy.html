🧸 Privacy Policy

Cozy Tidy Up - Educational Sorting Game for Kids

Effective Date: January 9, 2026

👶 Children's Privacy First: Cozy Tidy Up is designed for children and we take their privacy very seriously. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

1. Information We Collect

Cozy Tidy Up is designed to collect minimal to no personal information from users. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No Account Required: The app does not require registration or login.
  • No User-Generated Content: The app does not allow users to create, share, or upload any content.
  • Local Storage Only: Game progress (level completion) is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to any server.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

We display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of this app. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • General location data (country/region level)
  • App interaction data

Important: For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or when the app detects a child user, we request that AdMob serve only non-personalized, child-safe advertisements.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We recognize the importance of protecting children's privacy. Our practices include:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • We do not require any personal information to use the app.
  • All advertisements served are configured to be child-appropriate.
  • The app does not contain any social features, chat functionality, or external links accessible to children.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no user data stored externally. All game progress is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Deletion: Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to access or delete from our servers.
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.
  • Uninstall: You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
